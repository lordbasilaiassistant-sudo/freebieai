🎨 FreebieAI
Free AI image & video generation, right in your browser. No API key, no signup form, no backend to run. Powered by a multi-provider stack — FLUX, DALL·E 3, GPT-Image, Gemini & Grok for images; Sora 2, Veo 3.1, Wan & Seedance for video.

Video generation is heavier than images and may take 30–90s. Leave test mode on to preview the flow for free; turn it off for a real render (uses your Puter credits).

How it's free — no key, no signup: images & videos are generated through Puter.js, which uses a “User-Pays” model. There's no API key to paste and no signup form — on first use you click Continue once and an anonymous account is created automatically (no email, no password). Light usage is free. Everything runs client-side — prompts go straight from your browser to the model provider.
